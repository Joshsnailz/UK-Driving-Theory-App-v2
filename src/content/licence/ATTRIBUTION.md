# Content attribution

All theory questions in this app are derived from, and reference, rules in
*The Highway Code* and sign descriptions in *Know Your Traffic Signs*, both
published by the Department for Transport and licensed under the Open
Government Licence v3.0.

Each question carries a `highwayCodeRules` and/or `signId` field linking it
to the source material so that every answer can be verified against the
official text.

Required attribution (shown in-app on the *Legal & Licences* screen):

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
> Licence v3.0.
